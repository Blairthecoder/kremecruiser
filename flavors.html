Kreme Cruiser flavors

  • MysteryServer's choice. Kids love the gamble

Shaved ice, not a snow cone

A snow cone is crushed ice with syrup poured on top, which is why it runs out the bottom of the cone in about four minutes. Shaved ice comes off the block in fine ribbons that hold the flavor all the way through the cup.

That is why kids can carry a cup back to a table and it still tastes like the flavor they picked at the cart.

Common questions about the flavors

  • Dairy: none in any flavor, including Piña Colada and Orange Creamsicle.
  • Alcohol: none. Piña Colada is the flavor, not the drink.
  • Nuts: the cart does not carry nut products.
  • Mystery cup: the server picks the combination. It is a favorite with older kids.

If you have a specific allergy question, send it with your booking request and we will give you a straight answer before you commit.
